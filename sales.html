Repeatable Process

How I Sell

A disciplined outbound framework focused on thorough qualification, executive alignment, and continuous testing.

01

Research first

Research first: account and stakeholder mapping before outreach. I profile the account structure, identify key champions and economic buyers, and pinpoint company initiatives before crafting the first touch.

02

Discovery over pitching

Discovery over pitching: questions with senior buyers. Instead of leading with feature checklists, I run structured discovery with VP Sales, Directors, CMOs, and C-level buyers to uncover operational bottlenecks.

03

Test everything

Test everything: A/B testing messaging and CTAs weekly. Iterating subject lines, problem hooks, and friction-free CTAs every week lifted meetings booked by 35% and reply rates by 6%.

04

Multi-thread

Multi-thread: reach several decision makers, including C-level. De-risk deals and accelerate pipeline momentum by coordinating simultaneously across executive sponsors, department heads, and technical evaluators.
